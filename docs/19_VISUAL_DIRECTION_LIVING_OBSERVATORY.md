## Rules for clean but original composition
- Deliberate visual focus: 1 dominant scientific artifact, 1 compact action cluster, 1 source/uncertainty signal per screen.
- The flame is hero where relevant; don't put Earth, moon, large PIX, orbital station panorama, giant animated orange graph on EVERY screen.
- Research pages have little to no immersive background behind text; game pages have immersive scene with on-demand HUD.
- Animate changes between observation/analysis/evidence states, not constant ambient motion; respect reduced-motion.
- Distinctive visual signatures: apparatus cutaways, evidence chain ribbons, change-gravity morph, observation ruler, research note overlays, portal into real experiment records.
- No unauthorized NASA logo usage. Preserve first-party MicroFire style; no copied Exovisionaries art/structure/sound.
- Final verification: full page screenshots at 1440×900 and phone at ~390px; check contrast, overflow, focus, interactivity and render performance.
