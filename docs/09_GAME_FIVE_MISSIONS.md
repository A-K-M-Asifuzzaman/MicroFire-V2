# 09 — ORIGINAL GAME: FIVE ACTS, ONE CONSEQUENTIAL SCIENTIFIC CASE

Identity: **Flame Forensics / The Evidence Protocol**, led by PIX. Distinct from Moon rocket building: our player is a *fire investigation trainee*, not a rocket propulsion designer. One versioned case file persists. Real experiments are observed as recorded data; orbital station and lunar incident are illustrative fiction. Separation is mandatory.

## M1: The Unusual Signal (Station Arrival)
Playable objective: free movement/point-and-click exploration of orbit lab, follow emergency inquiry to correct observation terminal, inspect the NASA experiment source and identify what was actually observed. Control tutorial only a fraction of the mission. Failure: distractor panel suggests 'live fire alerts on Earth'; player must reject and open actual ISS study evidence. PIX responds to chosen action, not 20 lines of passive dialogue. Exit artifact: first sourced clue in Evidence Passport. Stage may be Coming Soon until built.

## M2: Reconstruct BASS-II (Apparatus Workshop)
Full compact, dimensionally checked 10-part NASA BASS-II educational apparatus: flow duct (7.6cm square x ~17cm; confirm from source), variable-speed fan, honeycomb straightener, nitrogen nozzle, sample holder, igniter, still camera, video camera, radiometer, copper exit plate. Use proper mechanical anchors/alignments and dependencies: duct before attached parts, holder before igniter, camera clear line of sight. Game fault tests: fan wrong direction, camera facing away, incorrect sample holder. User corrects and runs instrument verification, *not* a fake explosion. State of rig gates observation availability. Output: instrument qualification card with sources; explicitly not a certified lab build.

## M3: Flame Detective (Source-Based Test)
Choose an authenticated documented preset B20/B16/B19 or other valid record. Predict outcome; adjust exactly documented range; compare actual observation across tests without implying causality where conditions also changed. Player may choose invalid combination -> display extrapolation and explanation, not fabricated animation as result. Distinguish real footage, source-record output, authored visualization. Include auto-view preset and one optional challenge. Persist claim and supporting source.

## M4: The Claim Trial (Evidence Court)
Given three statements, some observed, some interpretation, one unsupported; collect source/page evidence, rank direct/analogous/mechanistic/gap, spot a misleading confident-sounding AI summary. Player must cite sources, identify mismatch and revise a conclusion. Gate is deterministic exact logic, flexible reason phrasing as secondary commentary. Outcome: source-backed scientific argument and clear uncertainty.

## M5: The Unanswered Question (Lunar Habitat Decision)
Fictional habitat event with oxygen/pressure/ventilation/material choices, explicitly *not a real NASA case*. Observe which evidence from orbit applies, mark gaps for lunar gravity/exploration atmosphere and question whether enough to assess safety. Correct answer can be 'insufficient evidence'; player proposes next measurement and non-certified precautions. Evaluate scientific justification, not arbitrary cinematic rescue. Export Evidence Passport showing decisions, evidence IDs, original study regimes, assumptions, remaining open questions.

## Gameplay uniqueness mechanics
- Evidence-to-action: each mission uses artifacts produced in previous one; state invalidates if underlying setup changes.
- Interactive errors have scientific cause and specific repairs, not generic game-over screen.
- Certified/certification language forbidden; `Evidence Passport` is an educational record only.
- PIX short contextual coaching; one clue at a time, captions, silent mode. No copied competitor personalities.
- Guided/Challenge modes share same scientific rules. No auto-build that bypasses observation/claim gates; optional visual demo has separate non-scoring session.
- Full campaign: save/resume, quick judge demo from fixture isolated from normal save, restart, snapshot revision migration, offline source snapshot, keyboard/touch support and retry.
