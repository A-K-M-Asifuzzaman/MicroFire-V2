## Laying the voice over the cut

Record one take per block. Then mux it without re-encoding the picture:
`ffmpeg -i MicroFire-local-240s.mp4 -i voice.wav -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k -shortest out.mp4`

If the video changes, rerun `tests/visual/video-edit.mjs` with the EDL; the timings above come from the same EDL.
