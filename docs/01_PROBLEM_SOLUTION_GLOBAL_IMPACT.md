# 01 — SCIENCE-FIRST PROBLEM, SOLUTION, AND GLOBAL VALUE

## The specific real problem, not a generic AI/space website
NASA's reduced-gravity flame studies are fragmented across experiment runs, PDF technical reports, images, video, platforms and differently reported conditions. A non-specialist can see attractive flame footage but struggle to determine what was actually measured; researchers can struggle to compare study regimes. Different gravity, oxygen, pressure, flow, material, sample geometry, and flame outcomes invalidate naive comparisons. Users may confuse observed results with simulated predictions and extrapolate from ISS conditions to Moon/Mars hazards.

## Our proposed solution: MicroFire Evidence Observatory
1. **Discover** — index authentic verified experiment records and supporting primary NASA sources with study/platform distinctions.
2. **Summarize** — extract auditable short explanations, every key statement linked to an original source record/page, plus a method/limitations panel.
3. **Rank** — rank evidence relevance for a *specific user question*; show the factors (dimensions matched and mismatched), never arbitrary '98% safety'. Prefer deterministic feature scoring before applying an LLM narrative.
4. **Interpret** — compare evidence cards side by side, explain variations and contradictory observations with provenance and uncertainty.
5. **Identify gaps** — an evidence coverage matrix; missing combinations get 'not established' not made-up predictions. Describe what next test might answer a question, not certify that test will be safe.
6. **Communicate** — one-click citation-backed, human-readable Evidence Passport / research brief, exportable as JSON/CSV/printable PDF once verified.
7. **Educate through interaction** — players assemble a faithful BASS-II apparatus, predict a real recorded outcome, challenge a misleading explanation, and investigate fictional Moon habitat risks without crossing beyond evidence.

## Worldwide audience and impact (without unsupported promises)
- Spaceflight safety researchers and mission designers: quickly compare documented source data and see scope/limits; not a safety-certified tool.
- Students/teachers anywhere: learn why microgravity flames differ and how to tell hypothesis from observation.
- International researchers: source-links, accessible exports, reproducible relevance criteria, shareable cases and low-bandwidth mode.
- Public communicators / safety educators: evidence-first flame literacy; explain transfer limits before applying Earth lessons. Do not claim a technology reduces real fire casualties without trials.

## Required end-to-end user stories
A: user selects ISS cabin ventilation conditions -> relevant BASS-II records ranked with exact matching/mismatching dimensions -> direct source -> compare -> honest synthesis -> share brief.
B: user asks about Moon gravity, 34% O2 at 56.5 kPa with PMMA -> show limited direct evidence and specific study regime gaps -> user creates a proposed research question with assumptions and sources.
C: teacher launches 3D lesson -> student installs BASS-II components -> a faulty camera cannot collect evidence -> repairs -> observes source-linked NASA result -> earns Evidence Passport.
D: scientist uploads nothing private, searches open NASA findings -> gets source-cited AI answer that abstains correctly when evidence insufficient.

## Evidence of our own impact: measure, never invent
Create a pre-registered evaluation protocol: timed task success for finding correct NASA BASS-II source vs direct browsing (participant study as available); citation precision/recall on locked test questions; model abstention precision; research question relevance rated by reviewers; accessibility and mobile time-to-interaction. Show sample size/method, uncertainty and limitations. No fabricated 'global users', 'saved lives', 'real-time ISS sensors', NASA partnership or 99% accuracy.

## Differentiation from Exovisionaries
Its engineering launch/build game can be evaluated independently; MicroFire's distinct technical moat is **evidence provenance + AI relevance + inter-study scientific uncertainty + a game where incorrect claims cause meaningful consequences**. Build better on measurable mission depth, accessibility, technical QA and scientifically anchored global value; never claim to beat a particular team or guarantee awards.
